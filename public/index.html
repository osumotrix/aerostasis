How the ramp works

Arrivals taxi onto a free stand. Fuel and catering can be done on the stand. Detailing and line checks need the aircraft in a hangar bay that fits its size.

  • Click an aircraft on the ramp, or in Flights, to see its services and send it to a bay or release it.
  • Drag an aircraft onto a hangar bay or stand to send a tug.
  • Release each aircraft before its ETD. Late departures cost fees and reputation.
  • The icons on the left open your goals, the flight list, the met report and the ramp log. Badges show when something needs attention.
  • Your crew are at the bottom of the screen. Click a face to see how they are doing and send tired people for a break.
  • Charter and front-desk requests appear on the right when they arrive.
  • The landing camera follows each arrival on final approach. Select Watch to see it full screen.
